## X / Twitter (thread version)

1/ Your phone is now a mic. 🎤

I built HypeBox, a push-to-talk app that sends your voice through any Bluetooth speaker, even while music is playing. 🧵

2/ The problem: parties, birthdays, workout classes and small events usually have a BT speaker and no PA. You can shout over the music or pause it and kill the vibe.

3/ How it works:
• Connect your BT speaker
• Hold the button (it glows with your voice)
• Talk. Your voice plays alongside the playlist, no pausing and no ducking
• Pick a 1–5s delay

4/ Built with React Native + Expo. Heading to the App Store and Google Play soon.

Want early access? Reply "HYPE" 👇
