Privacy policy

We collect nothing.

HypeBox does not collect, upload, store or share any of your data. This page explains exactly what the app does with your microphone.

Effective date: 25 September 2026
App: HypeBox for iOS (com.lesliepaulajayi.hypebox)

Who we are

HypeBox is made by Leslie Paul Ajayi, an individual developer. In this policy, "we" and "I" mean the developer.

What we collect

Nothing. HypeBox has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no crash reporting. It does not include any third-party SDKs that collect data. The app makes no network requests, so nothing you say or do in it ever leaves your phone.

Microphone

HypeBox needs microphone access to do its one job: play your voice through your Bluetooth speaker.

  • The microphone is only active while you are holding the button. When you let go, it stops.
  • Your voice is never uploaded, sent anywhere, or listened to by anyone other than the people in the room.
  • You can turn microphone access off at any time in iOS Settings > HypeBox > Microphone. The app will not work without it, but nothing else changes.

Temporary recordings

While you hold the button, HypeBox records your voice in short clips of 1 to 5 seconds (you pick the length). Each clip is saved to the app's temporary cache on your phone, played through your speaker, and then deleted straight away. Clips are not kept, backed up, or shared.

If the app is closed in the middle of a clip, that one clip can be left in the app's temporary cache. It never leaves your phone, iOS clears that cache automatically when it needs space, and deleting the app removes it.

Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any data, because we do not have any. No data goes to advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.

Children

HypeBox is not directed at children. Since the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If HypeBox ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change. If a future version were to collect any data, this policy would say so plainly before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on GitHub and I'll get back to you.
